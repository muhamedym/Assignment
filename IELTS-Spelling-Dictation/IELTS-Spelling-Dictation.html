American English sounds (IPA)

Every word in the app has its American pronunciation in IPA, the alphabet dictionaries use for sounds. This page shows what each symbol sounds like and how that sound is spelled in your words. Click any word to hear it.

How to read the IPA

ˈMain stress: the syllable after this mark is said loudest. /əˈbaʊt/ = aBOUT
ˌSecond stress: a little stronger than the rest. /ˌɛdʒəˈkeɪʃən/
·Syllable break in the spelling: ac·com·mo·da·tion
əThe most common English vowel: a short, weak “uh” in syllables that are not stressed. It can be spelled with any vowel letter, which is why it causes so many spelling mistakes.

How it works

  1. Choose what to practice. Listening, the 378 high-frequency words, Reading and Academic Writing have parts: pick one from the Part menu, or practice all of them. Then click Start. Each word is read twice. It is never shown or spelled out before you answer.
  2. Type the word and press Enter or click Submit. Capital letters and extra spaces don't matter, and hyphens are optional (you can't hear them). Letters and endings do matter.
  3. After every answer you see the word's meaning, part of speech (noun, verb…), word family and an example sentence, plus its American pronunciation (IPA), syllables, which letters make which sound, and any silent or double letters. Click 🔊 Hear to hear it again.
  4. The Sounds tab explains every IPA symbol and shows how each sound is spelled in your words, plus lists of silent-letter and double-letter words.
  5. If you're right, the next word starts after a few seconds (you can turn this off). Press Enter to go on sooner.
  6. If you're wrong, you'll see your answer and the correct spelling with the differences marked. Type the correct spelling and press Enter to continue. It still counts as incorrect.
  7. At the end you see your score and can retest the words you missed. Retests are scored separately.

Adding and removing words

Where your data is saved

Everything is stored on this device, in the app's own storage. Nothing is sent to any server and no account is needed.

  • Windows file version, moving the app folder: in Edge and Chrome, saved progress normally stays available, because the browser stores data for all local files together. Export a backup before moving anyway, just to be safe.
  • New phone, PC or browser: it starts empty. Use Progress → Export to save a backup file, then Import it on the new device.
  • Uninstalling the app (or clearing its data) deletes your progress. Export a backup first.
  • Clearing browsing data ("Cookies and other site data") or using InPrivate/Incognito windows deletes or doesn't keep progress.

About the voices

Voices marked local are installed on your device (Windows, or Google text-to-speech on Android) and work offline. Voices marked online (for example the "Natural" voices in Edge) send the word being spoken to Microsoft's or Google's speech service and need internet. No other data is sent. Your vocabulary lists and results are never sent.

No US English voice? Open Windows Settings → Time & language → Speech → Manage voices → Add voices, add English (United States), then close and reopen the browser.
