# Privacy Policy – Spelling Dictation

*Last updated: 9 November 2026*

This policy covers the **Spelling Dictation** app for Android (package `com.muhamedym.spellingdictation`) and the same app for Windows, made by Mohammed Mohammed ("I", "me").

**In short:** the app has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your words, answers, results and settings stay on your device. I don't receive any of your data.

## What is stored on your device

- Your practice history, missed words, settings and the words you add are saved only on your device, inside the app's own storage. They are not uploaded to me or to anyone else.
- Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes them.
- If backup is turned on for your Android device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup (for example Google Backup), under your device's backup settings.
- **Export progress** and **Export all words** create a file only when you choose to, saved where you choose.

## What leaves your device

1. **Online word look-up (optional).** When you add or import a word and **Look up online** is on, and the built-in dictionary is missing a detail, the app sends **only that word** to these free dictionary services: dictionaryapi.dev, Wiktionary (wiktionary.org), Wikipedia (wikipedia.org) and Datamuse (datamuse.com). The connection is encrypted (HTTPS). Nothing else is sent: not your word list, answers, results or any identifier. Like any website, these services see your device's internet (IP) address when the connection is made, and their own privacy policies apply. To stop this, untick **Look up online** in the Add word window.
2. **Speech.** Words and sentences are read aloud by your device's text-to-speech service (on Android, usually *Speech Services by Google*). Most voices work offline. If you choose a voice marked *online*, that speech service may send the text being spoken to its provider to create the audio, under the provider's privacy policy.
3. **Links** to websites open in your web browser.

## What the app does not do

- No sign-in or accounts, no advertising, no analytics or tracking.
- No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.
- Your data is not sold or shared.

## Permissions (Android)

- **Internet:** for the optional online word look-up and online voices.
- **Vibrate:** a short vibration after a wrong answer.

## Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for English exams. It is not directed at children under 13.
