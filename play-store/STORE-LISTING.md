## Full description (max 4,000 characters)

Spelling mistakes cost marks in IELTS Listening and Writing. Spelling Dictation for IELTS Practice helps you fix them the simple way: listen, type, and learn.

HOW IT WORKS
• Each word is read aloud twice. It's never shown before you answer.
• Type what you hear and tap Submit.
• Correct? See the meaning and move on.
• Wrong? See exactly which letters were wrong, then type the word correctly before moving on. Your first answer still counts, so your score stays honest.
• At the end, retest only the words you missed.

LEARN MORE THAN SPELLING
After every answer you see:
• the meaning
• the part of speech (noun, verb, adjective…)
• the word family (e.g. decide, decision, decisive)
• a natural example sentence

Not sure what you heard? Tap "Say a sentence" to hear the word used in context.

547 WORDS, READY TO PRACTICE
• Personal spelling priorities: commonly misspelled words
• Listening vocabulary: bookings, dates, numbers, travel, education
• High-frequency Listening words: school, shopping, work, health, travel, housing, weather and more
• Reading: science, environment, history, health, technology, psychology, business and society
• Academic Writing Task 1 and Task 2
• Mixed sessions that put your weak words first

Practice a whole section or just one topic.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Add, edit or delete words, and create your own lists
• Import words from a CSV or text file
• Choose the voice, speed and volume, with a slower option for difficult words
• Track your scores and missed words over time
• Export your progress as a backup

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your words and results stay on your phone, and the app works without an internet connection.
