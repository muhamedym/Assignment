Privacy Policy

Spelling Dictation for IELTS Practice · Designed by Mohammed Mohammed · Last updated: October 13, 2026

Summary

This app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

Data kept on your device

Your practice history, missed words, settings and any words you add are saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. They never leave your device unless you choose to export a backup file yourself. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Text-to-speech

Words are spoken using your device's own text-to-speech service (for example, Speech Services by Google on Android). If you choose an online voice, the word or sentence being spoken is processed by that voice provider under its own privacy policy. No other information from the app is sent.

Children

The app is a general study tool and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
